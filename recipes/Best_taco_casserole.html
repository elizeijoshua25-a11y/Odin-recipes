Best Taco Casserole

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 1 cup salsa
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup crushed tortilla chips
  • 1/2 cup sour cream

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a skillet, cook ground beef until browned. Drain excess fat.
  3. Add taco seasoning and salsa to the beef. Stir to combine.
  4. In a baking dish, layer the beef mixture, shredded cheese, and crushed tortilla chips.
  5. Bake for 20 minutes or until cheese is melted and bubbly.
  6. Serve with a dollop of sour cream on top.
